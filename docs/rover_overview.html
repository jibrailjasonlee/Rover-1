Rover-1

A two-wheel rover you drive from a phone browser. Each wheel has an encoder and its own speed loop, so it holds a speed and goes straight instead of drifting. A sonar on the front is meant to stop it before it hits a wall. Everything printed fits on a Bambu A1 Mini.

Sheet 2 · Why the motors hang under the plate

One number set the whole layout: 38 mm

The first layout put the motor brackets on top of the plate. The real caster is 34.5 mm tall, so that layout was never going to work. Flipping the brackets underneath made the numbers line up, and the wheel then needed a notch in the plate.

Sheet 3 · How it moves

Two wheels, two speeds, one turn

A differential-drive rover has no steering. It turns because one wheel goes faster than the other. The buttons use the same five commands as the firmware web page and the same speed constants, so the numbers below are what the code asks for.

Sheet 4 · Power and signals

Two bucks, because the motors are 6 V and the pack is not

The pack swings from 8.4 V full to 6.0 V empty. The motors are 6 V parts, so running them from the pack would overdrive them by up to 40%. One buck holds the motors at 6.0 V. A second holds the ESP32 and sonar at 5.0 V. Everything shares one ground.

Sheet 7 · What is still a guess

Open items before this is a finished robot

Motor can depthThe bracket pocket is 15.3 mm deep. How much of the 26 mm motor is can has not been measured with calipers.
Encoder resolution700 ticks per wheel turn assumes 7 pulses per motor turn and 100:1 gearing. Turn a wheel once in bench test 3 to find out.
Board mountsThe ESP32, driver and bucks have no mounts. The dashed boards show a layout that fits, not a design.
Insert fitThe listing says 4.2 mm outer diameter and the pilot hole is 4.2 mm. Heat-set pilots are normally a little smaller than the insert. Test one on the drilled part first.
ESP32 uploadThe board will not take an upload, most likely a charge-only USB cable. Nothing in the firmware has run on hardware.
Firmware gapsPID gains are placeholders that the sim says will chatter. No failsafe if the phone drops. Obstacle stop is an empty function.
Print orientationWith pads and a tall wall, the chassis needs supports somewhere. This is decided at the slicer, before the reprint.
Caster geometryThe caster drawn here is the retailer's STEP shape. Real height and swivel sweep get checked on the part.
